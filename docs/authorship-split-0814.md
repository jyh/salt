## Criterion caveats (ratification reserved to the Captain at freeze)

1. **Values-only files are counted per marker only.** `Salt/Chen/SuperProfileDef.lean`
   (570 lines) carries NO marker and NO generator basename mention
   (`ss2_profile.py` references `SuperProfile.lean` only, as a cross-check
   target); it enters the bracket TOP solely under the values-only criterion —
   whether script-computed values without a marker count as generated is the
   exact ruling reserved to the Captain at freeze (the `.v` adder8s boundary
   precedent). `Salt/Chen/SuperProfile.lean` (184 lines, markerless, mentioned
   by the generator) is the same class, surfaced by this run and left OUT of
   the bracket pending the same ruling.
2. **Mention ≠ generation.** The join lists every generator-dir script that
   names a tracked .lean file; most are CHECKERS reading their subjects
   (anchor_pin_check.py, docstring_coverage.py, tbal_ledgers/*, saltworks
   docs/ledger-tools/*). Emits-banner evidence exists only for
   `cbar_cert.py` → CbarCert.lean (both directions: the generator carries the
   banner text it writes, the output names the generator). SuperPanelsE/O join
   to `ss2_profile.py` in the banner direction (each output's marker names the
   generator).
3. **The 400-byte head check is invalid for .lean.** CbarCert's marker sits at
   line 30 (byte ~1204); the `.v` classifier's first-400-bytes rule returns a
   FALSE CLEAN — full-file scan is mandatory (docket §3b design finding).
4. **SuperPanelsE's scope is criterion-sensitive**: 43-line unmarked header;
   marker→EOF 7,875 vs whole-file 7,918. The bracket top uses whole-file per
   the docket's precedent.
5. **M4RowLinear.lean (11,267 lines): no marker, no generator references it** —
   unchanged at this run; remains agent-authored per the 8/14 docket finding.
6. **Never publish "~95% authored" from this alone** — the marker census bounds
   the SCRIPT-EMITTED share from below; it cannot certify authorship of the
   remainder (comment-line measurement is still owed, audit §A.3).

## Addendum 6, the same day: the Deuring–Heilbronn design question answered

Addendum 4 left `dh_repulsion_ordered` (`Salt/SW/TBalR8.lean`) and `dh_repulsion_tall_at` (`Salt/HB/CrownTheorem1.lean`)
as a design question. The answer is: do not compose them. Both quantify over q ≥ 2, χ primitive with χ² = 1 and χ ≠ 1, and a
real zero β₀ < 1, which is exactly the class `NoSiegelZerosAt cS` (`Salt/SW/StandoffGate.lean`) bounds by β₀ ≤ 1 − cS/log q for some 0 < cS.
Their right side is c·Q^(−b(1−Re ρ))/(log Q + 2)^k with Q = q(|Im ρ| + 2) ≥ 4, which at k = 1 is below c/log q. So under the
standoff both conclusions follow from the hypothesis on β₀ alone, with c := min(1/126848, cS) for `dh_repulsion_tall_at`
and b = k = 1, c := cS for `dh_repulsion_ordered`, and ρ unused. A standoff twin would read TWIN in this census and say
nothing about repulsion. This argument was read, not elaborated; the two rows stay in addendum 4's class, now with a reason.

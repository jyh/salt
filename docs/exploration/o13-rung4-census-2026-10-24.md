**Limits, beside the verdicts.** The census reads RESULTS and CAMPAIGNS only; a result with no row was not walked. The
TWIN-EXISTS test is a NAME heuristic and missed one twin (`siegelWalfisz_of_standoff`); a twin under an unrelated name

any other hypothesis or about effectivity.
